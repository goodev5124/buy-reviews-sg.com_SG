What exactly is a fake Google review?

A fake Google review is a comment published on a Google Business Profile that does not come from a genuine customer who actually used the service or product. In practice, the line is blurry: many businesses actively ask their satisfied customers to leave a review, which already amounts to a form of "prompted" review. Buying fake Google reviews simply takes this approach further by using professional writers.
