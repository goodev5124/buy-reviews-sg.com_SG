Digital word of mouth: Google reviews for hotels

Hotels used to rely on repeat guests and travel-agent recommendations. Today that recommendation has gone digital. When a traveller searches "hotel" on Google, the first results are those with the strongest star ratings. A hotel with 4.8 stars and 60 reviews will systematically be chosen ahead of a competitor with 3 reviews and 4 stars, even before the traveller looks at price.

Booking a room: when every star counts

Most hotel decisions come down to reassurance. Before booking a room, travellers scan reviews for cleanliness, location and staff friendliness. In these moments they choose the hotel with the best rating that appears in the results. Your Google reviews are therefore your best receptionist, working around the clock, 7 days a week.

Win back margin from the OTAs

Hospitality is a hyper-competitive sector, and OTAs take a heavy commission on every booking. A strong Google profile, backed by plenty of Google reviews for hotels, pushes your property to the top of Google search and Maps across Singapore. It's an investment that drives direct bookings and protects your margin from the platforms.
